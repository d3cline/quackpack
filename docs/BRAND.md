# Quackpack

Your links. Your photos. Your flock.

The mark is a pack of three ducks with the front duck quacking. The transparent PNG is at `assets/quackpack-logo.png`, used in the sidebar, sign-in screen, favicon and default ActivityPub profile icon. The wordmark is rendered as text so it stays legible at small sizes.

The logo was created with the built-in image generation tool. The PNG is included unchanged, with its transparency preserved.

## Generation prompt

+Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app logo symbol for Quackpack, a personal photo journal, link hub and Fediverse browser. Create one original, polished, compact emblem showing a pack of exactly three ducks together: a confident lead duck in front on the right with an open orange beak visibly quacking, and two friendly companion ducks nestled just behind to the left. Two small clean sound strokes near the open beak express the quack. Each duck remains a separate readable silhouette. Modern flat vector-style brand illustration, bold clean curves, minimal interior detail, warm and witty but mature. Palette coordinated with the app: warm ivory and mustard yellow ducks, dark charcoal #292a25 contours and eyes, burnt coral #df553a beaks, small muted sage #63755b accents. Strong silhouette readable as a small app icon. Centered square composition with generous safe margins. Truly transparent background. No text, no wordmark, no letters, no border, no background tile, no scene, no water, no gradients, no shadows, no photorealism, no mockup, no watermark. The only visual is the three-duck pack and their quacking sound strokes.
